# Exchange levers: merged proposal (2026-11-11, not approved, nothing built)

Sources: design_levers_a.md (Opus designer), Codex draft (scratchpad design_levers_b.txt), maps design_map_mod.md / design_map_vanilla.md.

Core idea (from A): Standing comes from TRUST and VOLUME; no lever raises both at once.
Test for every item: it changes how you play booms and panics, or rewards playing them well.

## Four levers
1. Exchange Rules: Open / Ordinary / Strict (A's lever 1 + Codex's "Supervise").
   Slow stance: 12 months to take effect, locked 3 years. Open = volume, banks get careless faster. Strict = trust,
   lending standards tighten, a little less credit. First lever that can tighten Lending Standards.
   Open question: vehicle. A = policy row in Banking; Codex = capital-state decree costing Authority.
2. Stand Behind the Exchange (A). Pledge to rescue the banks at every panic: cheaper automatic rescues, panics cost
   less trust, more flight money arrives. Breaking the pledge wipes trust.
3. Float a Great Issue (A). During a hot boom in a good: for 2 years more score and more bank exposure to that good;
   the trust result of how that boom ends counts double. Rewards a soft landing.
4. What the exchange is known for, earned by what you build (merge; replaces the one-off popup).
   Commodity Exchange <- Trade Centres; Share Market <- prosperous companies; Deposit Banks <- sound lending kept for years.

Dropped: Codex's "Protect households" as a lever (kept only as a possible vanilla link: Social Security softens bust radicals).

## Cross-links
- Financial Centres place gives more credit, only while Lending Standards are Sound or better (both drafts).
- Trust shrinks Market Mood swings and speeds recovery after a panic (both drafts).
- Company prosperity leans Market Mood up or down (Codex).
- Bad Debts freeze Standing until bought up (A).
- The World Centre's confidence nudges every other exchange's mood (A).
- Take Outstanding Credit out of the ranking score, so borrowing alone does not raise rank (Codex; brake on the runaway loop).

## Vanilla ties
In: Trade Centres, company prosperity, Laissez-faire (needed for Open rules), Social Security, Authority (if decree).
Out: International / World Centre give a little trade; Industrialists' strength (unverified modifier).

## Risks / must test in game
- Runaway loop: rank -> credit -> bigger booms -> score. Needs caps and a sweep.
- Exchange Rules next to Credit Policy may read as two overlapping dials.
- Unverified reads: company prosperity, company levels, institution levels, Industrialists modifier.
- AI use of each lever.
