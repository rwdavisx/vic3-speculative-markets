      How close this is to the game

      • Credit policy requires Central Banking and a six-month wait after a switch. The AI uses its temperament, governing interest groups, leverage and confidence to decide when to move.
      • By default each economy draws its budget, reserves, government debt and spare Authority together from recorded countries. Research follows the prerequisite tree and observed unlock dates. AI goods interventions use per-good Authority costs and subsidies. Rescue and loan buy-ups spend from the same treasury.
      • A Long Depression needs an eligible Great Power in an active panic. Its spread and recovery are approximations. Goods use bounded price episodes and the previous month's category trend. Player scenarios can specify an Exchange charter and credit policy. AI charter choices, contests, Great Issues, rivals, power blocs and Relief Works are outside the model.
      • Each candidate receives the same seeded input draws. Repeating a check with a different seed shows how much the results depend on the sampled economies and worlds.
