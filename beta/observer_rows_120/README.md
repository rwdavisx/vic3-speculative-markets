## Latest fixes and evidence

* The normal Competition tables, slot strip, contest bars, end dates and recent moves now use the viewed country. Observer mode hides actions. It reads outgoing and incoming lists for living counterparties and evaluates the contest script values from the observed side. The sector title plates use the vanilla expand-button texture. The setting that produced nine unsupported widget-margin errors is gone.
* During the beta.34 Brazil check in 1868, the normal Competition sections, a recent Bolivian press campaign, and the Country, Contest and Ends headings appeared in observer mode. Two defunct entries still drew blank flags and flooded the log during countdown evaluation. Beta.35 filters the display lists and guards the script values and queries against missing targets. The final populated rows, counts and absence of recurring errors still need live verification.
* Beta.35 passed the Python, generated-mod and CMF quick checks. All ten non-English languages generated with 3,182 current translations, zero outdated substitutions and zero English fallbacks. Their rendered tooltips have not all been visually reviewed. The CMF rebuild produced the same GUI bytes as beta.34.
* The Banking display groups the four largest qualifying goods exposures and any remainder in Other goods. Investor Interest uses neutral goods rows and sector title plates. Market supply by sector uses sell orders valued at base prices. Hover text for Favored and Shunned describes qualification, term, impact and strategic use.
* Rolling sentiment allows starts in any month after six consecutive positive-supply months at least 10% above or below base price. Small goods qualify. Persistence or an established share improves the odds. Each term lasts twelve months and changes Investor Interest by 12%, with up to three Favored and two Shunned goods. The offline tuner and focused regression tests cover the change. In the matched sample, skilled, passive and reckless strategies produced different modeled output and panic rates. See [the offline results](sentiment_matched_tuner.json). Country AI personalities and tuner personas are distinct unless their decision rules are explicitly mapped.
